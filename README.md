## Climate Change Awareness and Knowledge (Internal Code: MLD01e)

### Basic Logic
**Knowledge -> Awareness -> Action (-> Loss)**.             
Knowledge: Heard about Climate Change Dummy             
Awareness: Climate Change Awareness Dummy     
Action: ...         

### Standard Execute
- Tuning optimal hyperparameter
- Estimating the Importance of each variable
- Computing the average impact of each variable
- Visualization
         
### Expected Results
- Potential conclusion: The determinants of whether someone has heard about climate change may cluster around basic socio-demographics (education, age, media exposure, region).
- Knowledge as a Foundation: Individuals who have heard of climate change (knowledge) are significantly more likely to demonstrate higher levels of climate change awareness.
- Awareness as a Bridge to Action: Greater awareness of climate change is positively associated with a higher likelihood of engaging in climate-related actions.
